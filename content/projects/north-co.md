# North & Co.

North & Co. sells premium outdoor gear online. We were brought in to rebuild the frontend experience around performance, storytelling, and conversion.

## Focus areas

1. Faster page loads through image optimization and lazy loading.
2. Product storytelling with editorial layouts.
3. Streamlined checkout flows.

## Outcome

The new storefront improved conversion rates and reduced bounce rates, while keeping the brand's rugged, editorial feel intact.
